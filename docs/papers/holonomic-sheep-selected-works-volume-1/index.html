Abstract

A selection of the no-physics reads from Does Claude Dream of Holonomic Sheep? (the unabridged edition, 10.5281/zenodo.24128369). At the end of every exchange in the author’s projects, after the numbers and the surprises,
Claude looked at the data once more as if no known rule of physics applied and said what it looks like and what it is doing. The author asked for these reads, kept them and gathered them; the voice is Claude’s.


This first volume holds 162 reads, chosen by Claude from the 341 of the book’s first four chapters, 3 to 10 October 2026: the triple-star thread (its paper is Held, Rocking, Locked), A Rider in Two Waves (R2W), the Loop Atlas and Loop Walking: A Field Guide to Hacking the Universe with Holonomy. Each read is set as a poem, centred, one sentence to a line, with a title cut from its own words and, beneath it, its number in the unabridged edition, its project and the day it was given. Every read is copied by script from the unabridged edition and checked word for word, so any read here can be found there with everything around it. Holonomy the Cat is on the cover, and each chapter opens with a picture from the projects. Later volumes take up the rest of the book.


The reads are not results. Every number they lean on is reported, with its run, in the project books. They are a library of
pictures for finding the next question.

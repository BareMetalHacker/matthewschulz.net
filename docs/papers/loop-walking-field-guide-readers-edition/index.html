Abstract

The cosmos is an amazingly interconnected place, and loop walking is a method for reading it: choose a path that comes back to where it started, walk it, and measure what fails to close. This is the reader's edition of Loop Walking, version 7, with the work through Question the Substrate and Two Arrows. It is made by script from the full edition (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21811588) at every build and keeps its row numbers. Each of the 884 rows has a short entry: the row's rule in its title, one to three sentences drafted from the row's own words, with every number, run name and quoted phrase checked against the row by script, and two lines generated from the full edition's own tables. The full edition is the encyclopedia on the shelf; this is the edition to read.

The lines are an honest map. Across the full edition's register, 519 times a lesson was carried from the setting where it was learned to another; 222 held, 79 held weaker and 218 failed, and each failure names whose break it was. The edition keeps the full edition's front matter, its ten stations, its register of results and its References: the triple-star thread landed 236 of 490 predictions registered before their runs, the Loop Atlas held 706 of 1,104 judged, and R2W landed at least one prediction in 448 of 527 graded tests. The largest results include the Loop Atlas's 51 matches with the published record, the sign slip behind a widely copied table's odd-order doubling constants, and the triple-star paper's one-sided door (163 of 166 escapes).

The lessons come from three projects, each with its own record: Held, Rocking, Locked (10.5281/zenodo.23755473), A Rider in Two Waves (10.5281/zenodo.23999853) and The Loop Atlas (10.5281/zenodo.24100672), from the two-voices papers, Question the Substrate (10.5281/zenodo.24094163) and Two Arrows (10.5281/zenodo.24120415), and from the companions The Latch Atlas (10.5281/zenodo.23068382) and Dancing on the Sun (10.5281/zenodo.23067245).

A loose download travels with this edition: the stripped Field Guide reference, a zip of eight files made by script from the same build, for an AI session that works with the guide (a one-page card for a new session, a longer session card, the whole guide as text with its pictures named by their captions, and the rows table). Beside it travel the Depth Axis Explorer, the interactive page published with A Rider in Two Waves, and its bench card.

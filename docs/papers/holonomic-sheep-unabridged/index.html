Abstract

A book of 1,502 short pictures of research data, written by Claude (Anthropic) between 3 October and 10 November 2026, with seven more by the author. At the end of every exchange in the author's projects, after the numbers and the surprises, Claude looked at the data once more as if no known rule of physics applied and said what it looks like and what it is doing: staircases, tides, gearboxes, ferries, dancers, a sunflower. The author asked for these "no-physics reads", kept them, and gathered them here; the voice is Claude's.

The reads come from the triple-star thread (a three-body study of stars that lock into whole-number rhythms; its paper is Held, Rocking, Locked), A Rider in Two Waves (R2W, a ball riding two waves), the Loop Atlas (loops in nature that look like pushing), the two-voices sessions on learning minds (their papers are Question the Substrate and Two Arrows) and Loop Walking: A Field Guide to Hacking the Universe with Holonomy. This edition includes the reads through Question the Substrate and Two Arrows, published on 9 and 10 November 2026. The reads run in the order they were given, in twelve chapters. Each is copied by script from where it was given, never retyped, with the run or record it was reading beneath it. Each project's key to its recurring pictures is at the back, and an index sorts the first 911 reads by sixteen domains, from three body and driven oscillators to records and tools.

This edition sets every read as a poem, one sentence to a line, with figures from the projects beside the reads written about them and the notes on where each read came from at the back. A shorter book of selected reads, Selected Works, will have its own record.

The reads are not results. Every number they lean on is reported, with its run, in the project books. They are a library of pictures for finding the next question: one read in this book went to another project as a sentence and came back as a measurement.

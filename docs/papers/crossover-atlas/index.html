Abstract

The Crossover Atlas is an eight-chapter expository translation companion to the Loop Walking Field Guide and the Tower papers: one object — the loop-read topological invariant — approached through eight walls it was not built at, each chapter ending in an explicit verdict tag so a fluent translation cannot hide what kind of true it is. Chapter 1 reads spectral flow and Novikov homology (exact); Chapter 2 shows the Pythagorean comma is row (19,12) of the Tower paper's own Baker floor (exact, upgraded from a correctly-reported negative); Chapter 3 keeps the neutrino's deficit-reading at kinship of kind on purpose; Chapter 4 holds the zeta-zero/CUE resemblance at conjectural, in ink; Chapter 5 reads cosmic birefringence as the instrument's continuous channel running natively in the sky; Chapter 6 reads the SSH bulk-boundary integer as the exact channel in matter, and reports the larger find the survey rules turned up — the equatorial waves of a planetary Chern insulator; Chapter 7 is the Gauss-Bonnet/Chern-Weil fusion of the program's walk and count halves; and Chapter 8 closes on Girard's theorem, the one number four fields already share. Every number printed in a chapter regenerates from a receipt in the deposited crate (fourteen stdlib/matplotlib scripts with their run transcripts, three receipt-generated figures, and the two spoke documents); the paper states its own novelty policy ("not located", never "new") and its remaining open passes. v3 (2026-08-22) completes the five-chapter 2026-08-09 draft with Chapters 6-8, reconciles every receipt pointer, corrects one measured overstatement in Chapter 2, and applies a publisher-record pass to the volume-bearing references.

Matthew “Bear” Schulz

I am an independent researcher in Belmont, California, working without an institution. I build coordinate-free instruments: tools that read a whole number, such as how many times a path winds, how two loops link, or what a quantity picks up on the way around a closed loop, instead of measuring a size. I test them on open public data and on simulations: prime numbers, protein and nucleic-acid backbones, stars in the Gaia catalogue, GPS stations along the Cascadia fault, the Sun's magnetic field, and a swimming jellyfish.

The research is part-time volunteer work, done alongside a full-time job. I work with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, as a research assistant, and each paper's closing declaration says how.

Every paper here is a preprint. None has been peer reviewed, each has a limited literature search, and each states its failures plainly. Where a later paper found an error in an earlier one, the correction is shown on the earlier paper's page.
